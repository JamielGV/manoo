## 🇺🇸 English

### Tagline (one line)

**Manoo gives Claude Code hands.**

### Short pitch (WhatsApp / social, ~3 lines)

Manoo gives Claude Code hands: it sees your screen, moves the mouse, and
types for you. Tell it what to do and it does it — no clicking through
every step yourself. Runs 100% on your machine, nothing leaves it. Free
to try.

### Sales pitch (direct contacts, ~1 paragraph)

How many times do you repeat the same boring task on your computer —
filling out the same form, checking the same system, clicking through
the same ten spots? Manoo makes Claude Code do it for you: describe the
task once, and it watches the screen, moves the mouse, and types just
like you would. While it works, the screen splits so you keep seeing
your conversation with Claude, and the cursor glows neon so you know
it's Manoo moving, not you. You're always in control — touch the mouse
or keyboard and Manoo stops instantly. Free to try (25 actions per
session); if it's useful, Pro is $49 USD/year, unlimited.

### Sales bullets (for the page or a post)

- **Sees and acts** — doesn't just explain the steps, does them.
- **You're always in control** — stops the instant you touch anything.
- **100% local** — nothing about your screen leaves your machine.
- **You can see it working** — split screen + neon cursor while it acts.
- **Free to start** — Pro only if you actually use it often.

### Who it's for (useful for picking who to reach out to first)

- **New programmers and coding enthusiasts** — the main audience to
  promote to. Watching Claude Code actually move the mouse and type
  (split screen + neon cursor while it acts) is much easier to follow
  than reading terminal commands — you learn by seeing exactly what it
  does, not just reading text. Less intimidating for someone just
  starting out.
- Freelancers and agencies repeating the same manual task across
  multiple projects/clients.
- Anyone using Claude Code who gets frustrated describing clicks instead
  of just letting Claude make them.
- Small teams without budget for expensive enterprise RPA tools — Manoo
  is the light, free-to-start version.
